Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026 · Educational demonstration

This page describes how the Aqua Arcade coursework website handles demonstration data. The website is not a registered business and does not process actual orders or payments.

§ 1. Personal data administrator

Aqua Arcade is a fictitious student project. A real controller and registered address have not been designated. For the purposes of a public deployment, the project authors must supply valid data-controller information before collecting personal data.

§ 2. Processing purposes and legal bases

Shopping-cart contents and certain preferences are stored locally in your browser for demonstration functionality. The newsletter and contact forms do not send information to a backend service. Should the project start accepting real inquiries, its controller must define lawful purposes and bases for processing and provide applicable notices.

§ 3. User rights (GDPR / RODO)

Where personal data is processed by an actual controller, you may have the right to access, rectify, erase or restrict processing, to object, and to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority. You can remove this demo site's local browser data using your browser settings.
