✦ About Aqua Arcade

Aqua Arcade is an educational concept for a digital games storefront and gaming publication. Our aim is to bring together approachable game recommendations, useful guides and nostalgia for gaming history.

We create a welcoming space for people looking for their next favorite game, whether they enjoy relaxing simulators, exciting adventures or retro classics.
