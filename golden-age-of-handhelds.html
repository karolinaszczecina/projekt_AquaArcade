The Golden Age of Handhelds

From Game Boy to modern indie handhelds — a journey through portable gaming.

▦ 5 Apr 2026♙ Aqua Arcade Editorial Team◷ 7 min read

Gaming in your pocket

Portable consoles transformed gaming into something you could take almost anywhere. Small screens and bold ideas created unforgettable adventures.

Why handhelds felt special

Physical buttons, distinctive sounds and simple menus gave each device its own personality. Players learned to appreciate thoughtful design and compact games.

The spirit lives on

Modern portable systems and independent creators still build on that legacy, letting us enjoy classic adventures in new ways.
