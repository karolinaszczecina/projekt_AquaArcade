Subnautica: Beginner’s Guide

Remember when the digital world looked like a place full of possibilities? Blue skies, transparent interfaces, floating bubbles and endless green landscapes are making a comeback — and gaming culture is embracing them.

▦ 5 Apr 2026♙ Aqua Arcade Editorial Team◷ 6 min read

Welcome to the world beneath the waves. In Subnautica, the ocean is both your home and your greatest mystery: every dive can reveal useful materials, unusual creatures and places worth exploring. This beginner’s guide introduces a few simple ideas to help new players find their bearings.

The notes below are a friendly starting point for this demo article. Your own route through the underwater world may look completely different — and that is part of the adventure.

✧ Start with the basics

At the beginning, take a moment to look around your starting area and get familiar with the tools available to you. Move at a comfortable pace, keep an eye on your surroundings and collect only what you need for your next small goal.

A glimpse into the colourful underwater environments waiting to be explored.

Explore close to your starting point

It can be tempting to swim straight toward the most unusual landmark on the horizon. Instead, begin with nearby areas. Short trips make it easier to learn the environment, notice resources and return safely while you are still discovering how the game works.

“The ocean rewards curiosity — but a little preparation makes every expedition feel better.”

Make each dive count

Before leaving your base or starting area, decide what you want to find. A focused trip can be as simple as gathering a few materials, looking for a new route or observing an unfamiliar creature from a safe distance.

Beginner checklist

  • Get familiar with your basic equipment and inventory.
  • Explore nearby waters before travelling farther away.
  • Collect useful materials when you come across them.
  • Pay attention to your available oxygen and return with time to spare.
  • Use each expedition to learn one new part of the environment.

A simple expedition plan

StepWhat to focus onBeginner goal
1. Look aroundLearn the nearby environmentChoose a familiar route
2. GatherCollect useful materialsBuild up basic supplies
3. PrepareCheck equipment and resourcesPlan the next short trip

Curiosity is part of the journey

Subnautica is built around discovery. There is no need to uncover everything at once: take your time, experiment with the tools you find and let the ocean gradually become more familiar. Every small discovery can lead to a new question — and another reason to dive back in.
